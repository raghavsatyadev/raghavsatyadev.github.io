JobFinder

Be one of the first to apply. JobFinder checks your saved searches on LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor and a dozen other job boards, shows new jobs as soon as they are posted, has a local AI filter out the ones you don't want, and scores your resume against the rest. No account and no server: everything stays on your computer.

TVFreedom

A phone app that finds any Android TV on the Wi-Fi and turns off its ads, tracking and auto-installed apps over adb, using community-made debloat lists. You check every change first, a safety guard never touches what the TV needs, and Restore undoes it all. No computer or root needed.

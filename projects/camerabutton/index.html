CameraButton: your watch is the camera shutter.

A Wear OS app that turns the watch into a Bluetooth camera shutter. The watch pairs as a keyboard and presses the shutter in the phone's own camera app, so nothing is installed on the phone and you keep its camera's full quality and features.

What it does

No phone app

The watch pairs as a Bluetooth keyboard and remote, so any camera app works.

Self-timer

One-tap shutter, or a 3, 5 or 10 second timer.

Choice of key

Volume up, Volume down, Enter or Space, to suit the phone's camera app.

Stays connected

Keeps the link in the background, with an option to keep the screen on.

Try CameraButton

Free and open source. Install it from the latest release, or read and build the code.

OpenPlayer: a music player without Google services.

A fork of the PixelPlayerOSS music player focused on local playback, self-hosted libraries and an expressive Material 3 UI. It works offline by default, and online lookups stay off until you turn them on.

Source on GitHub All projects
ⓘ

Releases are on the way; until then, build it from source. It needs Android 11 or newer.

Features

What it does

Offline first

Plays your local library with no network at all.

Self-hosted streaming

Navidrome / Subsonic and Jellyfin are the only cloud sources.

Lookups you choose

LRCLIB lyrics and Deezer art stay off until you enable them; MusicBrainz runs only when you ask for it.

No Google services

No Firebase, Crashlytics, Play billing, Cast or Play Services dependencies.

Heading to Multiplatform

Moving to Koin and Room 3 ahead of a Compose Multiplatform port.

GPLv3

Free software, like the player it is forked from.

Try OpenPlayer

Free and open source. Read the code and build it from GitHub.

GrammarFix: fixes for your writing, and nothing leaves the phone.

A Grammarly-style writing assistant built in three days. It checks what you type in any Android app and offers fixes in a floating bubble. Every correction comes from an on-device Qwen3 model through llama.cpp: no server, no account, and your text never leaves the device.

What it does

Fixes in every app

An accessibility service reads the field you are editing and a small bubble shows each fix to accept or dismiss, or Accept all.

Rewrite in one tap

Polish, rephrase, shorten, make it friendly or formal, expand it, or give your own instruction.

Fully on-device

Qwen3 GGUF through llama.cpp. Fast mode answers in about 2 to 5 seconds. No network calls on the correction path.

Playground and tones

Try text inside the app, and edit or write the prompts behind each rewrite style.

Insights, kept private

Only counts and timestamps are stored, never your text. A test fails if anyone adds a text column.

Guided setup

Walks through the model download, Display over other apps and the Accessibility service.

Try GrammarFix

Free and open source. Install it from the latest release, or read and build the code.

Simple Cricket Umpire Scorer: tap once per ball.

An open-source, offline scorer for the umpire or scorer at a local cricket match. Tap once per ball, run or wicket; the app keeps the score, overs, run rates and the chase, and shows the result when the match ends. Rebuilt in Compose Multiplatform with Material 3 Expressive motion.

Screens

Features

What it does

Match list

Won, lost, draw and in-progress pills. Copy a match to start a rematch; deleting asks first.

Create a match

Date and time, team names, overs per inning, toss and batting order in connected button groups.

Scoring

Large run, ball and wicket buttons, an innings bar that turns wavy and red when the chase falls behind, and a celebration for wickets and every 50 runs.

Result

The winner's score with a slowly turning Material shape behind it.

Motion

Cards grow into their match screen, list items cascade in, and every animation stops when the device has animations off.

Offline

Everything is stored on the device with Room. No account, no network.

TVFreedom: a cleaner Android TV, from your phone.

TVFreedom finds any Android TV on your Wi-Fi and turns off its ads, tracking, auto-installed apps and pushy updates over adb, using community-made debloat lists. You check every change first, and one tap undoes all of it. No computer or root needed.

What it does

A faster TV

Fewer apps run in the background, so menus open faster and the TV has more free memory.

Fewer ads, less tracking

Turns off the maker's ad, promo and data-collection services.

No surprise apps

Stops the services that install apps you did not ask for, and optionally firmware updates that bring ads back.

Nothing breaks

A safety guard never touches what the TV needs (system, Wi-Fi, remote, inputs, Cast, voice). It saves a backup first, and Restore puts everything back.

Optional AI advice

Gemini Nano on the phone, Claude in Termux, the Claude API, or no AI at all. The built-in lists work fine for most TVs.

Works on most TVs

Android TV and Google TV sets from Xiaomi, TCL, Sony, Philips and Nvidia Shield, and Fire TV.

Try TVFreedom

Free and open source. Install it from the latest release, or read and build the code.
